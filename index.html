1. Introduction

Welcome to Serbio — Tanulj Szerbül ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a high-quality Serbian language learning experience while fiercely protecting your personal privacy.

✨ 100% Offline-First Guarantee: Serbio is engineered to operate fully offline. You can master vocabulary, take quizzes, listen to authentic pronunciations, and track your streaks without creating an account or sending data across the internet.

2. Information We Collect and Process

  • Account Data (Optional): If you choose to register for cloud backup, we collect your email address and custom display name via Supabase to synchronize your XP, streaks, and unlocked nodes across multiple devices.
  • Learning Progress: Completed lessons, review mistakes, and word mastery scores are stored primarily on your physical device using local storage (AsyncStorage).
  • Microphone & Audio Access: Zero Access We DO NOT record, store, or transmit your voice or microphone audio. Serbio does not request runtime microphone permissions. All Serbian audio in the app consists of pre-packaged audio clips played directly to your device speaker.
  • Background Services: Zero Background Tasks The application does not run background audio services or background location tracking when minimized.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We work exclusively with trusted, GDPR-compliant service providers solely to power core features:

  • Supabase: Provides encrypted cloud database storage and authentication protected by Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Google AI (Gemini Flash): Powers on-demand conversational dialogue exercises and grammar explanations. Queries sent to Gemini contain only linguistic context and zero personal user identifiers.
  • Expo / EAS: Delivers framework runtime updates and build distribution.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your local data resides in your device's private sandboxed app directory. Network communications are secured with industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal data with third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

5. Children's Privacy

Serbio does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details through an account creation, please contact us for immediate removal.

6. Right to Erasure & Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated cloud-stored progress at any time directly through the app's Profile settings or by emailing our developer contact below.
